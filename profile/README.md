## About

Royal Land is a Minecraft Bedrock community with its own game modes, events, and an active player base. Everything you play here is built by our team, from the gameplay to the website.

## Game Modes

| Mode | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **DonutSMP** | Survival multiplayer with an economy, trading, and a growing community |
| **BedWars** | Classic team battles: protect your bed, destroy theirs |
| **Football** | Real football matches inside Minecraft |
| **Roleplay** | Live a second life: jobs, roles, and a city full of stories |
